# Sources for the profile graphics

Each SVG in this folder is drawn for this profile. Places, numbers and lines come from the public pages below, as read on
17 October 2026. Map land comes from Natural Earth (public domain). Fonts embedded as subsets: Instrument Sans,
Instrument Serif and JetBrains Mono, all under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

| Graphic | What it shows | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `hero.svg` | 8 live and 35 planned sites; requests to the nearest live site | [polargrid.ai](https://www.polargrid.ai) homepage map, October 2026. The line under the name is Sev's. |
| `near.svg` | People 0–300 ms, nearby 364 ms, centralized 0.8 to over 1.6 s | Stivers et al., PNAS 106(26), 2009, as cited in [Why Voice Agents Need a Different Inference Stack](https://www.polargrid.ai/blog/why-voice-agents-need-a-different-inference-stack), 9 February 2026. 364 ms is that post's pre-production benchmark (p50); 0.8 to over 1.6 s is its estimate. |
| `failover.svg` | One site at a time goes dark, its traffic moves to the next nearest site | [polargrid.ai](https://www.polargrid.ai): "If a site goes down, its traffic moves to the next nearest site and the endpoint stays up." The outages are a drill drawn for the graphic. |
| `quadrant.svg` | The four archetypes and nine published placements | [economyofwisdom.org](https://economyofwisdom.org) home page; each placement is its field note's. Axes as on the Foundation's quadrant: amplification A across, filtering F up. |
| `voices.svg` | 22 of 51 named compassion, care or kindness | [economyofwisdom.org/research](https://economyofwisdom.org/research/), DOI 10.5281/zenodo.19582415, April 2026. |
| `world.svg` | Data-centre capacity: high-income 77%, low-income under 0.1% | World Bank, via [Field Note 12](https://economyofwisdom.org/blog/2026-08-02-open-weight-labs/), August 2026. Country income groups: the World Bank's current classification (api.worldbank.org, October 2026). |
| `compute-centre.svg` | The Compute Community Centre in section | [economyofwisdom.org/compute-community-centre](https://economyofwisdom.org/compute-community-centre/) and [/infrastructure](https://economyofwisdom.org/infrastructure/). |
